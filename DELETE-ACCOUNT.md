# Badal Exchange – Delete your account and data

This page is for users of the **Badal Exchange** app (Android, package
`com.badalexchange`). Follow these steps to ask us to delete your account and
the personal data linked to it.

## How to request deletion

1. **Withdraw your balance first.** An account that still holds money cannot be
   closed. Withdraw everything to your mobile-money number, or ask support to
   help you do it.
2. Open the app and go to **Profile → Support**, then choose **WhatsApp**,
   **Telegram** or **Facebook**.
3. Send the message **"Delete my account"** from the **phone number you
   registered with** (we use that to confirm it is really you).
4. We confirm by message and delete the account **within 30 days**. You will
   be told when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your name, phone number and password (hash), and your login.
- Your identity-verification (KYC) data: document type and number, date of
  birth, expiry date, country, and the photos of your document and selfie.
- Your notification (device) token and your app settings on our servers.
- Your wallet and your support messages that identify you.

## What we keep, and for how long

Records of money movements are kept because financial-record and anti-fraud
rules require it. They are kept **without your name or identity documents**
(your account is anonymized) for **5 years** after the transaction, and then
deleted. This covers: the amount, date, payment method, transaction status and
reference, and the phone number or platform account ID used for the payment.

## Partial deletion

You can also ask support to delete some of your data without closing your
account (for example your identity photos). Send the request from the same
registered phone number.

See also our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
