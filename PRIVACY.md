# Badal Exchange – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026
**App:** Badal Exchange (Android, package `com.badalexchange`)

Badal Exchange ("we", "us") lets you add money to your wallet and withdraw it
through mobile money (EVC Plus, Golis, ZAAD, eDahab) and betting-platform
accounts. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it
is shared with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account:** your full name, your phone number (+252) and a password. The
  password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
- **Transactions:** the deposits and withdrawals you make: amount, payment
  method, the phone number or betting-platform account ID you pay from or to,
  status, time and transaction reference.
- **Identity verification (KYC), only if you choose to verify:** document type
  and number, full name, country, date of birth, expiry date, and photos of
  your identity document and a selfie.
- **Support:** if you contact us (for example on WhatsApp from the app), the
  details you send. When the app opens WhatsApp for account approval it
  prefills your customer ID, phone number and platform account ID. It never
  includes your password, PIN, one-time codes or ID images.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Notifications:** a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can
  tell you when a deposit or withdrawal completes.
- **Fonts:** the app may download fonts from Google, which receives your IP
  address like any website would.
- **Your settings** (language, theme) are stored on your device.

We do **not** use advertising, we do **not** track you across other apps, and
we do **not** sell your data.

## 2. Permissions

- **Internet:** to talk to our servers.
- **Notifications:** to tell you a payment completed.
- **Camera / photos:** only when you choose to take or pick photos for
  identity verification. Photos are sent only when you submit the form.

## 3. How we use it

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To process your deposits and withdrawals and to credit your wallet exactly
  once per payment.
- To prevent fraud, money laundering and abuse, and to verify your identity
  before large or first withdrawals.
- To send you the notifications and messages you ask for, and to give support.
- To keep records we are required to keep, and to improve reliability.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Payment providers:** to complete a payment we send the amount and the phone
  number or account ID involved to the mobile-money network or betting
  platform you chose (for example EVC Plus / Hormuud, Golis / Sahal,
  Telesom / ZAAD, eDahab).
- **Our service providers:** Supabase (secure hosting of our database and
  login) and Google Firebase (notifications). They process data for us under
  their own security terms.
- **Our staff:** authorized agents and administrators see the details they
  need to confirm payments and verify identity. Identity documents are visible
  only to authorized staff and each view is logged.
- **Authorities:** when the law requires it, or to protect you and others from
  fraud.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and transaction records while your account is active and
afterwards for as long as financial-record and anti-fraud rules require.
Identity documents are kept while your account is active and as long as the
law requires, then deleted.

## 6. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Access to data is limited by
role and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password
private and never share one-time codes.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, and to close your
account.

**How to delete your account and data:** contact support from the app
(Profile → Support: WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook) from the phone number you
registered with and ask us to delete your account. Your wallet must be empty
(withdraw any balance first). We delete or anonymize your personal data, except
transaction records we must keep by law, and confirm to you when it is done.

## 8. Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from
anyone under 18; if you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will
delete the account.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version;
important changes will also be shown in the app.

## 10. Contact

Use the Support options inside the app (Profile → Support), which open our
WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook contact.
